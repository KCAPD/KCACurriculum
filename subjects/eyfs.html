Our Early Years

Confident, curious and ready to thrive

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King's Cross Academy, we believe the Early Years should give every child an exceptional start to their education. Our ambition is for children to leave Reception as confident, curious and increasingly independent learners who communicate well, build positive relationships and have the knowledge, skills and attitudes they need to thrive in Year 1 and beyond.

Our Early Years curriculum is ambitious, inclusive and carefully sequenced across the seven areas of learning. High-quality adult-led teaching sits alongside purposeful play, exploration, repetition and rich first-hand experiences so that children can learn, practise and apply new knowledge with growing independence.

Communication and language sit at the heart of the curriculum. Reading, stories, songs, rhymes, sustained conversation and deliberate vocabulary development build the foundations for later learning, while mathematical understanding grows through explicit teaching, meaningful practice and mathematical talk.

Our six KCA values — Courage, Respect, Kindness, Integrity, Endurance and Aspiration — are woven through relationships, routines, stories, play and experiences. High expectations, strong relationships with families and thoughtful adaptation ensure every child can access the same ambitious curriculum.
