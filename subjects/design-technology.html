Design & Technology

Designing, making and evaluating purposeful products.

Our Design & Technology curriculum

Ideas made purposeful

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, Design & Technology helps children identify problems, develop ideas and create purposeful solutions. Our ambition is for pupils to see themselves as designers: curious about how and why things work, willing to experiment and able to use knowledge creatively in response to real needs.

Our curriculum is built around four connected elements: Designing, Making, Technical Knowledge and Evaluating. Children consider purpose and users, generate and communicate ideas, select tools and materials, and build increasingly sophisticated understanding of structures, mechanisms, electrical systems, textiles and other technical concepts.

Design is taught as an iterative process rather than a set of instructions leading to a predetermined product. Children test ideas against their purpose and criteria, identify strengths and areas for improvement, solve problems and adapt when something does not work as expected.

Every child is entitled to practical, ambitious design education. Explicit modelling, carefully sequenced instruction, appropriate tools and materials, vocabulary support and repeated practice reduce barriers while preserving the independence, creativity and decision-making at the heart of successful design.
