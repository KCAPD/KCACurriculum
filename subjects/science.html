Science

Developing scientific knowledge, enquiry and understanding of the natural world.

Our Science curriculum

Curiosity, evidence and explanation

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, we want every child to see themselves as a scientist. Science enables pupils to understand the world around them, ask questions about what they observe and develop the knowledge and ways of thinking needed to seek explanations.

Scientific knowledge is deliberately built over time across biology, chemistry and physics. Alongside this substantive knowledge, children learn to work scientifically by asking questions, observing, classifying, measuring, investigating, identifying patterns, analysing evidence and communicating findings.

Practical science is carefully chosen to deepen understanding rather than simply provide activity. Children learn that explanations should be supported by evidence and that results may challenge an original idea, requiring scientists to question, reconsider and refine their thinking.

Every child has opportunities to investigate, handle equipment and encounter the natural world. Explicit teaching, models, visual representations, structured discussion and vocabulary support reduce barriers, while links with the scientific community around King’s Cross connect classroom learning with science as a living discipline.
