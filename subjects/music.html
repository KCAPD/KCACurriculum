Music

Developing musicianship through listening, composing and performance.

Our Music curriculum

Listening, creating and performing together

At King’s Cross Academy, music is a powerful form of expression that enriches children’s lives, nurtures creativity and connects people. Our ambition is for every child to see themselves as a musician: able to listen with understanding, perform with confidence, create music and enjoy making it with others.

Musicianship develops through the connected disciplines of listening, performing and composing. Children encounter music from different periods, traditions, cultures and genres while progressively building understanding of pulse, rhythm, pitch, dynamics, tempo, timbre, texture, structure and notation.

Singing, instrumental performance, improvisation and composition allow children to apply knowledge practically and creatively. They learn that becoming a musician involves listening carefully, practising deliberately, responding to feedback and returning to a piece repeatedly in order to improve.

Every child has opportunities to sing, play instruments, compose, listen and perform. Appropriate instruments, technology, modelling and structured rehearsal reduce barriers, while KCA’s wider musical offer ensures access to high-quality music-making does not depend on circumstances or prior experience.
