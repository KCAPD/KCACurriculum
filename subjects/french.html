French

Developing confidence and curiosity in another language and culture.

Our French curriculum

Language as connection, curiosity and confidence

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, we want every child to experience the excitement and satisfaction of communicating in another language. Through weekly French throughout Key Stage 2, children develop the knowledge, confidence and curiosity to understand and express themselves beyond the languages they already know.

Listening, speaking, reading and writing are deliberately connected. Children build vocabulary, phonics, grammatical knowledge and language structures progressively, regularly retrieving and reusing what they have learned in increasingly varied contexts.

We want pupils to move beyond repeating predetermined phrases. As their knowledge grows, children select and adapt vocabulary and structures to express their own ideas, ask and answer questions, notice patterns between languages and communicate with increasing independence.

KCA’s linguistic diversity is a significant strength. Clear modelling, visual support, repetition and structured rehearsal reduce barriers, while a culture that values having a go helps children develop the courage and endurance required to communicate in an unfamiliar language.
