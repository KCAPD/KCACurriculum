Our Computing curriculum

Understanding, creating and shaping a digital world

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, Computing gives children the knowledge, skills and confidence to understand, create and participate in an increasingly digital world. We want pupils to become curious, capable and responsible users of technology who can shape the digital world rather than simply consume it.

Our curriculum develops children’s understanding across computer science, information technology and digital literacy. They learn about algorithms, programming, data and computer systems while also using digital tools purposefully to create, organise and communicate content.

Computational thinking is built progressively. Children learn to identify patterns, break problems into smaller parts, test ideas, debug and improve solutions. Online safety is woven through Computing and the wider curriculum so that pupils learn to assess risk, protect information and make informed, ethical choices.

We recognise that children’s access to technology outside school varies considerably. Every child therefore has meaningful opportunities to use technology, develop computational thinking and build transferable knowledge that remains useful as devices and applications continue to change.
