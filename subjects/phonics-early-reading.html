Building confident, fluent readers from the very beginning.

Our approach to phonics and early reading

This page is being prepared for the curriculum website launch in September 2026. It will clearly identify the systematic phonics programme used at King’s Cross Academy and explain how early reading develops through EYFS and Key Stage 1.

This page will include

  • the systematic synthetic phonics programme used at KCA;
  • how phonics develops from Nursery through Reception and Key Stage 1;
  • how reading books are matched to pupils’ developing phonic knowledge;
  • assessment, additional practice and keep-up support;
  • information about the Year 1 Phonics Screening Check; and
  • resources and guidance for families.
