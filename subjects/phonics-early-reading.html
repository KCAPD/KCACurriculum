Phonics & Early Reading

Developing confident readers, writers, speakers and listeners.

Our Phonics & Early Reading curriculum

Every child becoming a reader

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, we believe every child can become a reader. Learning to read unlocks the wider curriculum, increasing independence and opening the door to a lifetime of stories, knowledge and ideas; our ambition is to develop both successful readers and children who genuinely love reading.

We use Little Wandle Letters and Sounds Revised from Nursery onwards. Nursery children follow Little Wandle Foundations, developing careful listening, phonological awareness and sensitivity to sounds through language, stories, songs and rhymes before systematic teaching of the alphabetic code begins in Reception.

Daily phonics teaching is explicit and carefully sequenced. Children learn phoneme–grapheme relationships, blend to read and segment to spell, with regular retrieval and books closely matched to taught knowledge. Repeated reading develops accuracy, automaticity, fluency, expression and comprehension.

Reading extends far beyond decoding. Adults share ambitious stories, poems, rhymes and non-fiction so children encounter rich vocabulary and ideas from the beginning. Assessment identifies gaps quickly and targeted practice supports our ambition that children keep up rather than have to catch up.
