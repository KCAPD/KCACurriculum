Our English curriculum

Finding a voice through language and literature

At King’s Cross Academy, we believe language gives children the power to understand the world, communicate with others and express who they are. Our ambition is for every child to leave KCA with a lifelong love of reading, the confidence and skill to write with purpose, and the ability to communicate ideas clearly and effectively.

Oracy is central to learning. Children learn to articulate ideas, explain their thinking, listen attentively, ask questions and respond thoughtfully. Rich vocabulary and purposeful opportunities for discussion, presentation and performance help children adapt spoken language to audience, purpose and context.

Reading sits at the heart of our curriculum. Systematic phonics builds successful decoding before fluency, comprehension and rich discussion allow children to focus increasingly on meaning. Carefully chosen fiction, non-fiction and poetry provide both mirrors of children’s own experiences and windows into lives, places, cultures and perspectives beyond them.

Writing is taught as purposeful communication. Secure transcription, vocabulary, grammar and sentence knowledge give children the tools to plan, compose, reread, edit and improve for meaningful audiences and forms. High expectations and thoughtful adaptation ensure every child can develop an increasingly confident and individual voice.
