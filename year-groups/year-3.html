Autumn 1 Big Question

How has London's past shaped the city we know today?

Learning presentationOpen Afternoon
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Year 3 begins this enquiry with London Exploration Day (second half of Autumn 1). Pupils then build connected knowledge through History: history of London, Science: forces and magnets, DT: mechanisms – model bridge with moving parts. Horizon 22 extends the learning through first-hand experience. The unit returns to Integrity as pupils respond to the Big Question and share their learning through Open Afternoon.
