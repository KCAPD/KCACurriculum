Autumn 1Big Question

Who am I?

Authentic audienceParents: Showcase learning environment & children’s learning
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Nursery begins with children settling into their new environment and exploring who they are. Through talk, mark making, play and familiar routines, children begin to make sense of their own life story and family history, grow in confidence with others and develop the foundations for communication, early writing and mathematical thinking.

KCA ValueIntegrity
Authentic audienceParents: Showcase learning environment & children’s learning
Trip / visit / experienceSettling-in period

Early writing

Taking part in mark-making in different ways; sometimes giving meaning to marks; beginning to show a dominant hand preference.

Phonics & mathematics

Phonics: Phase One – environmental sound discrimination · Maths: comparison, shape and space, repeating patterns, and hearing and saying number names.

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: growing vocabulary and own life story/family history · EAD: pretend play and exploring materials · PSED: confidence in new social situations and interest in other children’s play · CLL: following three-key-word instructions, short adult-led attention and asking for help · PD/PE: movement, balancing, riding and ball skills.

Autumn 2Big Question

How does our community celebrate?

Authentic audienceParents: Nativity
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Children explore how their community celebrates and begin to notice similarities and differences between people. A local-area experience helps connect Nursery learning to the community around school, while children strengthen pencil control, listening, counting and participation in shared routines and celebrations.

KCA ValueRespect
Authentic audienceParents: Nativity
Trip / visit / experienceLocal area

Early writing

Giving meaning to marks; developing a dominant hand preference; using a pencil with some control.

Phonics & mathematics

Phonics: Phase One – instrumental sound discrimination · Maths: ordering number names, subitising 1–3, repeating patterns, and exploring position and space.

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: positive attitudes to differences and talking about celebrations · EAD: drawing from memory, representing ideas and singing favourite songs · PSED: responsibility and membership of the Nursery community · CLL: five-minute adult-led focus, simple instructions and questions · PD/PE: skipping, hopping, balancing and large-muscle mark making.

Spring 1Big Question

What will we find in the woods?

Authentic audienceSLT
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Forest school gives children a first-hand context for curiosity, talk and exploration. They investigate the world around them, use their senses, build increasingly complex play and develop the endurance to sustain attention, communicate ideas and keep working through practical challenges.

KCA ValueEndurance
Authentic audienceSLT
Trip / visit / experienceForest school sessions

Early writing

Talking about drawings and mark making; beginning to form some letters of their name.

Phonics & mathematics

Phonics: Phase One – body percussion · Maths: subitising, counting and labelling 1–3, shape and routes, and exploring patterns.

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: countries and differences children have experienced or seen · EAD: complex small-world stories, detailed drawing and using materials and tools · PSED: cooperative play, solving conflicts and talking about feelings · CLL: short exchanges, 4–6 word sentences and expressing feelings · PD/PE: matching physical skills to tasks, group activities and movement sequences.

Spring 2Big Question

How can we take care of living things to help them grow?

Authentic audienceParents
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Children consider care, kindness and responsibility as they explore their environment and develop increasingly cooperative play. Their cultural experience at Little Angel Theatre broadens storytelling and expression, while communication, early writing, rhyme and mathematical problem solving continue to build.

KCA ValueKindness
Authentic audienceParents
Trip / visit / experienceLittle Angel Theatre

Early writing

Beginning to form letters of their name correctly; talking about drawings and what their mark making says.

Phonics & mathematics

Phonics: Phase One – rhythm and rhyme · Maths: taking and giving 1–3, shape and measure, talking about dots, comparing and sorting collections.

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: using senses to explore and comparing materials · EAD: developing ideas, responding to what they hear and pitch matching · PSED: following rules, empathy and cooperative friendships · CLL: sustained conversation, talk to organise play, eight-minute attention and prediction · PD/PE: choosing resources and collaborating to manage large items.

Summer 1Big Question

How can we show courage?

Authentic audienceReception Children
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Children develop confidence, risk taking and openness to new experiences as they consider what courage can look like. A farm visit provides a real context for learning about plants, growth and life cycles while children strengthen fine-motor control, mathematical thinking and imaginative expression.

KCA ValueCourage
Authentic audienceReception Children
Trip / visit / experienceFarm

Early writing

Using a comfortable pencil grip; forming some letters of their name correctly; explaining their drawings and mark making.

Phonics & mathematics

Phonics: Phase One – alliteration and voice sounds · Maths: leading repeats, puzzles, making patterns, subitising through games and actions, and showing five.

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: planting seeds, caring for plants, life cycles and respecting living things · EAD: imaginative small worlds, emotions in drawing and colour mixing · PSED: assertiveness, emotional vocabulary and confidence to try new things · CLL: wider vocabulary, prepositions, two-part instructions and simple why questions · PD/PE: one-handed tools, controlled pencil grip and dominant-hand preference.

Summer 2Big Question

How does knowing where our food comes from help us make good choices?

Authentic audienceParents: Art Show
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Nursery concludes by connecting the natural world, food and healthy choices. The Floating Garden / Skip Garden gives children a purposeful first-hand context for observing plants and natural materials as they consolidate early writing, sound awareness, counting, comparison and growing independence.

KCA ValueAspiration
Authentic audienceParents: Art Show
Trip / visit / experienceThe Floating Garden / Skip Garden

Early writing

Producing recognisable letter shapes in their name and using some print and letter knowledge in early writing.

Phonics & mathematics

Phonics: Phase One – oral blending and segmenting · Maths: creating patterns, counting to five, and matching, sorting and comparing.

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: changes in cooking or melting, observing animals and plants and exploring natural materials · EAD: joining materials, exploring textures, controlled instruments and improvised songs · PSED: independence in care needs and healthy choices · CLL: expressing a point of view, ten-minute adult-led focus and joining songs and rhymes · PD/PE: increasing independence in dressing and undressing.
