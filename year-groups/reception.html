Reception

Building confident, curious and increasingly independent learners

Digital curriculum bookReception2026–2027 curriculum
Across the year

Six purposeful learning journeys.

Reception learning is organised around six KCA values-led questions. Across the year, children develop communication, early reading, writing, mathematics, understanding of the world, creative expression, physical development and personal, social and emotional development through purposeful teaching, play and first-hand experiences.

Autumn 1Big Question

Who am I?

Authentic audienceParents
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Reception begins with children exploring who they are, the people important to them and their place within the school community. Forest school sessions provide a rich setting for talk, observation and shared experiences. Children develop the earliest foundations of meaningful writing, begin Phase Two phonics, explore matching, sorting, measures and pattern, and talk about their own lives, families and similarities and differences between people.

KCA ValueIntegrity
Authentic audienceParents
Trips / experiencesForest school sessions

Literacy, phonics & maths

Writing carries meaning; early marks, letter-like shapes and familiar letters · Phonics: Phase Two · Maths: matching, sorting, comparing, measures and pattern

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: my family, my school and my world; similarities and differences · EAD: artistic effects and pretend-play storylines · PSED: self, family & relationships · CLL: listening, storytimes, rhymes, poems and songs · PE: First PE

Autumn 2Big Question

How are celebrations the same or different?

Authentic audienceParents
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Children investigate celebrations and the different ways people mark important occasions. Through talk, stories and first-hand experiences, they compare traditions while developing respect for similarities and differences between people. Forest school continues and a visit to St Pancras Old Church extends learning beyond the classroom. Writing becomes increasingly connected to phonics, while mathematics develops secure understanding of numbers to five and shape.

KCA ValueRespect
Authentic audienceParents
Trips / experiencesForest school sessions · St Pancras Old Church

Literacy, phonics & maths

Writing: giving meaning to marks and linking sounds to letters · Phonics: Phase Two, including digraphs · Maths: 1, 2, 3; circles and triangles; 1–5; shapes with four sides

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: celebrations, Diwali, Christmas and international cultures · EAD: refining ideas, representing learning and group singing · PSED: Celebrating Differences · CLL: asking questions, developing social phrases and using new vocabulary · PE: Gymnastics

Spring 1Big Question

How can we explore our world?

Authentic audienceReception Children
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

The spring term begins with exploration. Children use simple maps, investigate places and cultures and develop the language needed to describe what they notice. Local area trips give this enquiry a real-world context. Their writing becomes more purposeful, Phase Three phonics expands the sounds they can use, and mathematics develops understanding of five, mass, capacity, six, seven, eight, length, height and time.

KCA ValueEndurance
Authentic audienceReception Children
Trips / experiencesLocal area trips

Literacy, phonics & maths

Writing with a purpose; hearing and recording key sounds; simple CVC words · Phonics: Phase Three · Maths: Alive in 5; mass & capacity; Growing 6, 7, 8; length, height & time

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: simple maps, international cultures and British culture · EAD: collaborative creation and singing · PSED: Belonging · CLL: asking and answering questions, listening in a group, well-formed sentences and connectives · PE: Fitness and Fundamental Skills

Spring 2Big Question

How can we take care of living things to help them grow and change?

Authentic audienceParents
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Children turn their attention to living things, growth and change. An in-school baby duckling experience gives them an immediate opportunity to observe, care, talk and ask questions. They learn to identify features of spring and use increasingly precise vocabulary to describe life cycles. Writing develops into simple sentences, phonics learning is reviewed and extended into longer words, and mathematics builds secure understanding of nine, ten and 3D shape.

KCA ValueKindness
Authentic audienceParents
Trips / experiencesBaby Ducklings (in-school experience)

Literacy, phonics & maths

Writing: wider range of words, tricky words and simple sentences · Phonics: review Phase Three GPCs and longer words · Maths: Building 9 and 10; explore 3D shapes

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: spring and life cycles, including vocabulary such as tadpole, cocoon and chrysalis · EAD: listening and responding to music · PSED: Growing and Changing · CLL: describing events, listening in a group and applying new terminology · PE: Multi Skills

Summer 1Big Question

How courageous are community helpers?

Authentic audienceReception Children
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Children explore the people who help us and the roles they play within the local community. A Fire Station visit gives direct insight into community roles, while a visit to Little Angel Theatre broadens children's cultural experiences. Writing becomes more accurate and sustained, Phase Four phonics supports longer words and endings, and mathematics moves beyond ten towards twenty, composition, sharing and grouping.

KCA ValueCourage
Authentic audienceReception Children
Trips / experiencesFire Station · Little Angel Theatre

Literacy, phonics & maths

Writing: simple sentences, core tricky words, multisyllabic words and word spacing · Phonics: Phase Four · Maths: To 20 and beyond; How many now?; manipulate, compose & decompose; sharing and grouping

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: people who help us and roles in the local community · EAD: dance and performance art · PSED: Keeping Safe & the local community · CLL: retelling familiar stories and using learned language · PE: Olympic Dance

Summer 2Big Question

How does knowing where our food comes from help us make good choices?

Authentic audienceParents
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Reception concludes by tracing food from where it grows to the choices people make about eating and preparing it. A visit to the Local Community Garden gives children a purposeful connection to growing, food and the local environment. Children apply increasingly secure writing and phonics knowledge, make mathematical connections and use vegetables and fruit to explore simple food preparation and healthy choices.

KCA ValueAspiration
Authentic audienceParents
Trips / experiencesLocal Community Garden

Literacy, phonics & maths

Writing at greater length with phonetically plausible and core-word spellings, capital letters and full stops · Phonics: Phase Four review and consolidation · Maths: visualise, build and map; make connections; consolidation

Wider EYFS learning

Understanding the World: how food gets to our plate; vegetables, fruit and simple meals · EAD: music making and dance · PSED: Healthy habitats & choices · CLL: using new vocabulary and selected non-fiction · PE: Outdoor Adventure
