Autumn 1 Big Question

How do our experiences shape who we become?

Learning presentationOpen Afternoon
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Year 2 begins this enquiry with Special item show and tell and identity self portrait. Pupils then build connected knowledge through History: the history of Camden – people and events, Science: animals including humans – basic needs, Art: sculptures inspired by King’s Cross Estate. Walk along the Canal workshop · ChickenShed Water Safety theatre show extends the learning through first-hand experience. The unit returns to Integrity as pupils respond to the Big Question and share their learning through Open Afternoon.
