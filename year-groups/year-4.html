Autumn 1 Big Question

Why is it our responsibility to care for the natural world?

Learning presentationOpen Afternoon
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Year 4 begins this enquiry with Nature Day: exploring nature across London and Essex (second half of Autumn term). Pupils then build connected knowledge through Science: states of matter, History: the Mayans, Art: painting and printmaking – Mayan masks. St Pancras Gardens extends the learning through first-hand experience. The unit returns to Integrity as pupils respond to the Big Question and share their learning through Open Afternoon.
