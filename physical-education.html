Physical Education

Building physical confidence, teamwork and lifelong participation.

Our Physical Education curriculum

Confidence, competence and a lifelong love of movement

At King’s Cross Academy, Physical Education is essential in helping children develop healthy, active lives, physical confidence and a positive sense of wellbeing. Our ambition is for every child to discover ways of being active that they enjoy and can continue throughout their lives.

Children experience a broad range of activities including athletics, gymnastics, dance, games, outdoor and adventurous activity and swimming. Fundamental movement skills are deliberately built before being applied and combined with increasing control, fluency and precision.

PE also develops knowledge about bodies, health and wellbeing alongside teamwork, leadership, fair competition, perseverance and personal improvement. Swimming is a distinctive entitlement: every year group from Reception to Year 6 takes part in intensive sessions that progressively build water confidence, technique and water safety.

We have high expectations for every child. Activities, equipment, instruction and support are adapted to reduce barriers without lowering expectations, while KCA’s wider sporting offer gives pupils with different strengths and levels of confidence opportunities to participate, represent the school and experience success.
