Geography

Exploring places, people, environments and our interconnected world.

Our Geography curriculum

Making sense of an interconnected world

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, Geography helps children understand the world around them and their place within it. Our ambition is for pupils to develop rich knowledge of places, people and environments, understand the connections between them and remain curious about a world extending far beyond their immediate experience.

Children build secure locational and place knowledge alongside an understanding of the physical and human processes that shape our world. Important concepts including place, space, scale, environment, interconnection and change are revisited and deepened over time.

Children also learn to think and work as geographers: asking questions, interpreting maps and information, collecting and analysing data, identifying patterns and communicating what they discover. Fieldwork is central, with King’s Cross and London providing a rich environment for observation and investigation.

Our diverse community is a powerful starting point, but we deliberately broaden every child’s horizons. Maps, photographs, models, first-hand experiences, visits and explicit vocabulary teaching help pupils develop the knowledge and confidence to make sense of an interconnected and changing world.
