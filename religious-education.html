Religious Education

Exploring beliefs, traditions and lived experiences with respect and curiosity.

Our Religious Education curriculum

Understanding belief, worldview and lived experience

At King’s Cross Academy, Religious Education helps children understand the beliefs, worldviews and experiences that shape people’s lives. Our ambition is for pupils to become knowledgeable and curious about religion and worldviews and confident discussing questions of belief, meaning and identity with respect.

Children build knowledge of religious and non-religious worldviews through beliefs, practices, stories, traditions, communities and lived experiences. Learning is deliberately sequenced so important ideas are revisited, connected and understood with increasing sophistication.

Our curriculum recognises diversity both between and within traditions. Children encounter sacred texts, artefacts, places of worship and celebrations while learning that people who share a worldview may interpret and practise it differently. Discussion and reflection help pupils ask thoughtful questions and listen respectfully when perspectives differ.

RE does not tell children what they should believe. It provides knowledge and space to encounter significant questions and develop their own thinking. We never assume prior experience or ask a child to speak on behalf of a religion or worldview; careful resources, vocabulary and discussion ensure every pupil can participate.
