Our PSHE curriculum

Knowledge, values and voice for life

At King’s Cross Academy, PSHE is fundamental to preparing children to lead happy, healthy and fulfilling lives. We want pupils to understand themselves, build positive relationships, make informed decisions and participate successfully in the diverse society in which they are growing up.

Our curriculum progressively develops knowledge of relationships, physical and mental health, safety, the wider world, rights and responsibilities, including statutory Relationships and Health Education. Important ideas are revisited as children mature so they can approach increasingly complex situations with greater understanding and confidence.

Children learn about healthy relationships, boundaries, wellbeing, risk, trusted adults, online safety, diversity, money, the environment and their role within communities. Discussion and reflection develop their voice: pupils learn to express a perspective, explain it, listen carefully and disagree respectfully.

Our six KCA values provide a shared language for choices, relationships and responsibilities. Safe, inclusive teaching never requires children to disclose personal experiences, while thoughtful adaptation ensures essential knowledge and safety messages are understood by every child.
