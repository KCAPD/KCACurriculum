Mathematics

Building fluency, reasoning and confident mathematical problem solving.

Our Mathematics curriculum

Confident, connected mathematical thinking

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, we want every child to see themselves as a mathematician. Mathematics helps children make sense of patterns, relationships and problems, and our ambition is for pupils to become confident, flexible and independent mathematical thinkers.

Learning is carefully sequenced so new ideas build upon secure prior knowledge. Conceptual understanding, fluency, mathematical reasoning and problem solving work together, with practical resources, representations and models helping children see the structures and relationships within mathematics.

Children develop fluency through purposeful practice and learn to explain how they know, identify patterns, justify their thinking and tackle unfamiliar problems. Mathematical talk and explicitly taught vocabulary give pupils the precision to communicate ideas and respond to the reasoning of others.

We have high expectations for every child. Carefully chosen representations, manipulatives, explicit modelling, additional practice and precise support reduce barriers without lowering expectations, while pupils who grasp concepts rapidly deepen understanding through reasoning, connections and increasingly complex problems.
