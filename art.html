Art

Developing creativity, visual literacy and confidence through artistic practice.

Our Art curriculum

Creative thinking made visible

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, Art & Design gives children a powerful way to observe, understand and respond to the world. Our ambition is for every child to see themselves as an artist: confident in expressing ideas, willing to experiment and equipped with the knowledge and skills to turn creative thinking into something meaningful.

Our curriculum deliberately develops both technical skill and artistic independence. Children build an increasingly secure repertoire across drawing, painting, sculpture and other art, craft and design media, while learning to observe closely, investigate materials, generate ideas and make purposeful creative choices.

Children encounter artists, craftspeople and designers from different times, places, cultures and traditions. Sketchbooks are central to this journey, giving children space to explore, practise, record, reflect and refine as an initial idea develops towards a final piece.

We have high expectations for every child. Teaching, materials, tools and support are adapted to reduce barriers without limiting creativity or lowering expectations, ensuring all pupils can develop their own artistic voice and experience ambitious art education.
