British Sign Language

Developing inclusive communication and understanding of Deaf culture.

Our British Sign Language curriculum

Communication that connects our community

Read the full Vision & Intent →

At King’s Cross Academy, we believe every child should recognise the value of communication in all its forms. Our co-location with Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children gives BSL an authentic purpose: it is a living language used within the community children share every day.

Our BSL curriculum is carefully sequenced from Nursery to Year 3. Children build vocabulary, language structures and communication skills progressively, revisiting and applying signs so that they become increasingly confident and fluent in meaningful interactions.

Children learn that BSL is a language in its own right, with its own grammar and structures, and develop an age-appropriate understanding of Deaf culture, community and identity. They learn to attend to handshape, movement, location, orientation, facial expression and body movement as features through which meaning is communicated.

BSL is inherently visual and physical, offering another way to understand and express meaning. Clear modelling, repetition, visual cues and frequent practice help every child participate successfully while developing Respect, Kindness and the courage to communicate in a language that may initially be unfamiliar.
