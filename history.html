History

Understanding people, events and change through evidence and interpretation.

Our History curriculum

Connecting the past to the world we inhabit

At King’s Cross Academy, understanding history helps children make sense of the world and their place within it. We want pupils to develop secure and connected knowledge of the past, appreciate its complexity and remain curious about how people, places and societies have changed over time.

Children study significant people, events, societies and periods from British and wider world history. Knowledge is deliberately built and revisited through concepts including chronology, continuity and change, cause and consequence, similarity and difference, and historical significance.

Alongside knowing more about the past, children learn how historical knowledge is constructed. They ask increasingly sophisticated questions, examine evidence, consider the usefulness and limitations of sources, compare accounts and recognise that interpretations can differ.

Our location makes history tangible. King’s Cross, London and the museums, buildings and cultural institutions around us connect classroom learning with the historical world beyond school, while careful adaptation and explicit vocabulary ensure every child can access the same ambitious curriculum.
