Autumn 1 Big Question

Why is it so important to know where our food comes from?

Learning presentationOpen Afternoon
Explore this unit +
The learning journey

Year 1 begins this enquiry with Food tasting – different crops grown in Kenya. Pupils then build connected knowledge through Geography: maps of a farm, Science: animals including humans – senses, DT: food technology – smoothie. Kentish Town City Farm extends the learning through first-hand experience. The unit returns to Integrity as pupils respond to the Big Question and share their learning through Open Afternoon.
